# v14.1 targeted upper-level refinement (beta)

- Preserves v14 scoring for passages without sufficient specialized academic evidence.
- Adds grouped editorial concept cues for evaluation, governance, distribution, epistemic reasoning and qualification.
- Adds conditional advanced-level floors only when multiple independent signals coincide (specialized concepts, academic expressions, clauses, sentence complexity and sufficient length).
- Adds recognized specialized expressions to the explanation and diagnostic scores.
- Does **not** claim official TOPIK calibration or full Korean morphological parsing.
- Requires browser testing on previous and fresh unseen passages; thresholds are experimental.
